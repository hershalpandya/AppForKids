# Sample source review and answer keys

The two invented worksheet pages let you practise the review before using private schoolwork. [Page 1](worksheet-page-1.png) · [Page 2](worksheet-page-2.png) · [Parent guide](../../parent-handoff.md)

Use the parent prompt from the guide with both pages. The exercise practises counting two collections and comparing their quantities using « plus » and « autant ». The P1 mathematics reference covers this comparison vocabulary and counting collections of up to 20 on pp. 31–32. [Fédération Wallonie-Bruxelles mathematics reference](https://ifpc.cfwb.be/v5/documents/tc/refMATH.pdf#page=31)

## Source review and clarification

| Source | Reading | Draft decision |

| Page 1, A | 5 above and 3 below. The upper row has more. | Use it as question 1 in both revisions. |
| Page 1, B | 4 above and 4 below. The quantities are equal. | Use it as question 2 in revision 1. |
| Page 2, C | 3 above and 5 visible below, with a mark over the next position and a note reading « 5 ? ». The exact quantity below is uncertain. | Ask whether the mark covers a sixth counter. Set this item aside until the parent confirms the quantity. |
| Page 2, D | Add two counters to a drawing. | Leave it out because the prompt asks for comparison. |

For this rehearsal, send the following reply after playing revision 1.

> On page 2, item C, the mark covers a sixth counter. There are three on top and six below. Please use C for question 2 and keep question 1. Let me check the new answer and hint in a fresh preview.

The resulting revision 2 uses item C for question 2 and keeps the game ID `les-rangees-a-comparer`.

## Revision 1 answer key

The worked example shows 2 counters above and 1 below. Both questions display « Où y a-t-il le plus de jetons ? » with the choices **En haut**, **En bas** and **Autant**. [Revision 1 JSON](les-rangees-a-comparer-v1.json)

| Question | Top and bottom rows | Correct answer | Success feedback | Retry hint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Page 1, A | 5 and 3 | En haut | Bravo ! En haut, 5 jetons. En bas, 3 jetons. | Compte la rangée du haut, puis celle du bas. |
| 2 · Page 1, B | 4 and 4 | Autant | Bravo ! Il y a 4 jetons dans chaque rangée. | Compte chaque rangée. Trouves-tu le même nombre ? |
